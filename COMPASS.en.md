# Sensory Compass

While another player or mob is using a doll bound to you, right-click with a regular compass to bind it to that user. Binding works independently of the second-camera setting and supports custom doll animation packs.

The target position updates every half second. The binding follows the target across dimensions, but the vanilla compass can point correctly only while you are in the target's dimension. Offline players retain their last known position. Mob death removes the binding; unloaded mobs retain their last known position.

Put a bound compass through a grindstone to remove its binding. Unrelated custom data from other mods is preserved.

Offhand doll: right-click opens the doll menu after main-hand, block, and entity interactions have had a chance to consume the action. Sneak-use preserves normal actions. Pressing M no longer shows the main-hand doll warning; NoN's own entry points still require a doll in the main hand.

The interface and compass messages follow Minecraft's language setting, with Simplified Chinese and English translations. Animation names supplied by authors and custom player names remain as written.
